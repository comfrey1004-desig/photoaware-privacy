PhotoAware Privacy Policy

PhotoAware

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

PhotoAware (the "App") is an application designed to help users inspect metadata contained in photos before sharing them and, when desired, remove information that may reveal personal information, such as GPS location data, date and time taken, camera information, and device information.

PhotoAware's Photo Privacy Principle
Photos selected by the user and the analysis and removal of EXIF/GPS metadata are processed locally on the user's device. The PhotoAware developer does not transmit or store those photos or their EXIF/GPS metadata on developer-operated servers.

1. Processing of Photos and Metadata

Photos explicitly selected by the user and metadata contained in those photos, including EXIF data, embedded GPS location data, date and time taken, and camera or device-related information, are processed to identify potential privacy risks and provide metadata removal functionality.

The analysis and removal of photo metadata are performed locally on the user's device.

The PhotoAware developer does not transmit or store user-selected photos, EXIF information read from those photos, or GPS metadata embedded in those photos on developer-operated servers.

2. Removal of Photo Metadata

When the user chooses to remove personal information, the App creates a new copy of the photo with its orientation correctly applied and metadata that may contain personal information removed.

The user's original photo is not directly modified or overwritten during this process.

3. Saving Photos

When the user explicitly chooses the save function, a sanitized copy of the photo may be saved to the user's device gallery.

Photos saved to the device can be viewed and deleted directly by the user through the device's gallery or other photo management applications.

4. Photo Sharing and Temporary Files

PhotoAware uses the Android system sharing interface (Share Sheet).

A photo is provided to the sharing process only when the user explicitly initiates the share function. The user then chooses the destination application through the Android system Share Sheet.

During the sharing process, a temporary photo file may be created in the App's private cache area so that the photo can be provided to the Android sharing system.

The App attempts to delete the temporary file after the sharing process is completed. If the App terminates unexpectedly, a temporary file may remain for a period of time. Such files are stored in the App's private cache area and may be removed through operating system storage management or by clearing the App's cache.

5. Developer Servers and User Accounts

PhotoAware currently does not provide user registration or user account functionality.

The developer also does not operate a server for storing users' photos or EXIF/GPS metadata analyzed from those photos.

6. Location Information

PhotoAware does not request device location permission for the purpose of determining the user's current location.

GPS metadata that already exists within a photo file is processed locally on the device only for the purpose of identifying potential privacy risks and, when requested by the user, removing that information.

7. Access to Photos and Device Features

PhotoAware uses the Android system photo picker to process photos explicitly selected by the user.

The App does not use camera functionality and does not request location permission to determine the user's current location.

8. Advertising Services and Third-Party SDKs

PhotoAware uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide advertising within the App.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as IP addresses, app usage and interaction information, diagnostic information, advertising identifiers, and other device-related identifiers for purposes including advertising, ad performance measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Such advertising-related data is processed in accordance with Google's policies.

PhotoAware does not provide user-selected photos, EXIF information read from those photos, or GPS metadata embedded in those photos to Google AdMob.

For more information about how Google processes information, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy

9. Sale and Sharing of Information

The PhotoAware developer does not sell user-selected photos, EXIF information analyzed from those photos, or GPS metadata embedded in those photos.

The developer also does not provide such photos or photo metadata to Google AdMob or other third parties for advertising purposes.

However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the App may independently process data required to provide advertising services in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and applicable policies.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

The PhotoAware developer does not store or retain user-selected photos or EXIF/GPS metadata analyzed from those photos on developer-operated servers.

Photos that the user explicitly chooses to save are stored on the user's device. The user can delete those photos directly through the device's gallery or another photo management application.

11. Children's Privacy

PhotoAware is not designed for the purpose of intentionally collecting personal information from children.

Matters related to the App's target age group and advertising are handled in accordance with applicable Google Play and Google AdMob policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, third-party services used by the App, or applicable laws and policies change.

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised content and effective date will be made available on this page.

13. Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding PhotoAware's privacy practices or this Privacy Policy, users may contact the developer using the developer contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
